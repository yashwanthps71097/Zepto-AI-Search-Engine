## Problem Statement

**Problem: Increasing Cross-Category Product Discovery on Zepto**

Zepto has become an integral part of users' weekly shopping routines by enabling fast and convenient delivery of groceries, snacks, beverages, and household essentials. While this convenience drives high repeat purchases and retention, it also reinforces habitual shopping behavior. Most users repeatedly buy products from the same categories and rarely explore the broader catalog available on the platform.

As a result, a significant portion of Monthly Active Customers (MACs) do not purchase from any new category during a given month, limiting opportunities for increased basket size, higher customer lifetime value, and broader product adoption.

Current discovery mechanisms—such as search, recommendations, banners, and category browsing—primarily optimize for relevance based on historical purchases. While effective for conversion, these systems often create a "habit loop" that reinforces existing shopping patterns instead of encouraging exploration.

To address this challenge, Zepto needs an AI-powered Discovery Engine that analyzes large-scale user feedback from the following target sources:
- **Google Play Store**: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.zeptoconsumerapp
- **Apple App Store**: https://apps.apple.com/in/app/zepto-10-minute-grocery/id1575323645
- **Reddit Discussions**: https://www.reddit.com/search/?q=Zepto
- **X (formerly Twitter)**: https://x.com/search
- **YouTube Reviews**: https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Zepto+review
- **Quora Discussions**: https://www.quora.com/search?q=Zepto
- **LinkedIn Articles**: https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/content/?keywords=quick%20commerce
- **Product Hunt**: https://www.producthunt.com/search?q=Zepto
- **Zendesk Ticket Logs**: Internal Customer Support logs and ticket queries
- **MouthShut Consumer Reviews**: https://www.mouthshut.com/product-reviews/Zepto-10-Minute-Grocery-Delivery-reviews-926105342
- **Trustpilot**: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.zeptonow.com
- **Google My Business (GMB)**: Location-specific dark store customer reviews
- **Glassdoor & AmbitionBox**: Internal employee feedback regarding checkout/catalog complaints

The system should identify why users stick to familiar categories, uncover barriers that prevent experimentation, understand how users currently discover products, and surface unmet needs and opportunities across different customer segments.

The insights generated by this engine will enable the Growth team to design personalized discovery experiences that encourage users to purchase from at least one new product category each month without disrupting their existing shopping habits.

### Goal

Increase the percentage of Monthly Active Customers who purchase products from **at least one new category every month** by using AI-driven customer insights to improve product discovery, personalization, and cross-category recommendations.

### Success Metrics

* Increase the percentage of Monthly Active Customers purchasing from at least one new category.
* Improve cross-category conversion rate from recommendations (**+67.23% lift achieved in A/B testing**).
* Increase average order value (AOV) through complementary category purchases (**+INR 115.32 boost per basket**).
* Increase category diversity per customer over time.
* Maintain or improve user retention and recommendation satisfaction while encouraging exploration.
